# Orthogonality screen — locating the (1,1,0) leak

> **Directive** — Attribute the leak to a specific interaction rather than to
> the gate as a whole.

## Approach

Pairwise: each regulator against each non-cognate promoter, one interaction per
well. Nine combinations, four replicates.

Single-interaction resolution is the point. A whole-gate measurement can only
say the gate leaks; it can never say which part to change.

## Result

[C0062 (LuxR)](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/C0062_CD/C0062_CD.gb) activates
[R0040 (pTet)](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/R0040_AB/R0040_AB.gb) at 6
percent of its cognate activity. Nothing else exceeds 1 percent.

That single interaction accounts for the entire (1,1,0) excess measured in entry
12 — an independent confirmation, not a fitted explanation.

## Options

Swap [C0062 (LuxR)](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/C0062_CD/C0062_CD.gb) for
an orthogonal activator, at the cost of re-characterising an input; or accept a
26-fold gate with one asymmetric state. Entry 15 needs the LuxR input
specifically, so the swap is deferred and the asymmetry documented.
