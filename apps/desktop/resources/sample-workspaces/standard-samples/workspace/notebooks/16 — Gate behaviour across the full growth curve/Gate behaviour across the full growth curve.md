# Gate behaviour across the full growth curve

> **Directive** — Test the charter criterion that mid-exponential performance is
> retained into stationary phase.

## Method

Every 20 minutes from lag through 2 hours into stationary. Same eight input
states as entry 12.

The single measurement most circuit papers omit, and the one the charter singled
out: a gate characterised only at mid-exponential is characterised in its best
conditions.

## Result

26-fold at mid-exponential falls to 17-fold at stationary onset — 65 percent
retained, just above the 60 percent criterion.

The loss is asymmetric: the OFF state rises while the ON state is roughly flat,
consistent with reduced repressor turnover rather than with reporter
accumulation.

## Interpretation

Criterion met, but with little margin. Any change that raises the OFF state —
including the entry-06 read-through, if a future build abandons divergent
orientation — would push this below criterion.
