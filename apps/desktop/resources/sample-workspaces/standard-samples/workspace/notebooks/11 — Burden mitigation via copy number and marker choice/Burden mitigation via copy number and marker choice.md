# Burden mitigation via copy number and marker choice

> **Directive** — Buy back growth rate without spending the dynamic range the
> charter protects.

## Levers considered

Copy number: [DVA_AE](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/DVA_AE/DVA_AE.gb) and
[DVA_AF](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/DVA_AF/DVA_AF.gb) (higher) against
[DVK_AE](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/DVK_AE/DVK_AE.gb) and
[DVK_FG](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/DVK_FG/DVK_FG.gb) (lower).

Marker: ampicillin versus kanamycin. Beta-lactamase is itself a secreted,
well-expressed protein and is not a free choice.

## Result

Moving the output unit to the lower-copy
[DVK_FG](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/DVK_FG/DVK_FG.gb) recovers 9 of the 14
percent growth deficit and costs 4-fold of the 34-fold ON/OFF ratio — leaving
22-fold, still above criterion.

Switching marker alone gave no measurable benefit and was dropped.

## Decision

Lower copy number is adopted. Entry 12 builds the three-input gate on
[DVK_FG](../../sequences/kits/moclo-cidar/DVK_FG/DVK_FG.gb).
