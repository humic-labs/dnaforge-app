# Plasmid retention and functional stability over 100 generations

> **Directive** — Establish whether the gate survives serial passage, or only
> survives a single overnight.

## Method

Serial passage, 1:1000 daily, 10 days, roughly 100 generations, with and without
selection. Retention by plating; function by re-assaying the eight input states
at days 0, 5 and 10.

## Result

Under selection: 97 percent retention, ON/OFF 26-fold falling to 24-fold.
Stable.

Without selection: 41 percent retention by day 10, and the retained population’s
ON/OFF falls to 6-fold — worse than plasmid loss alone predicts, indicating
enrichment for cells carrying a functionally degraded gate.

## The finding that matters

Selection-free operation does not merely lose the plasmid; it selects for broken
versions of it. Any deployment outside selection needs chromosomal integration,
which is out of scope here and recorded in entry 18.
